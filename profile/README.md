## About Nodalia

**Nodalia** is an independent technology initiative creating software for people who value freedom, performance and thoughtful design.

From operating systems and native desktop applications to engineering tools and artificial intelligence, our projects share a common philosophy:

**Powerful technology should be accessible, efficient, reliable and enjoyable to use.**

We believe great software is more than a collection of features. It is the result of careful engineering, coherent design and attention to the smallest details.

Our applications are built as independent products, with cross-platform compatibility and open technologies at their core.

## Our philosophy

**Design with care.**  
Every interaction matters. Software should be intuitive, cohesive and enjoyable to use.

**Engineering without shortcuts.**  
Maintainable architecture, meaningful testing and technical correctness guide our development.

**Performance is a feature.**  
We aim to build responsive, resource-efficient applications that respect your hardware.

**Freedom by design.**  
Open standards, interoperability, transparency and user choice are fundamental.

**Independent, yet connected.**  
Each application should stand on its own while sharing a consistent design language and working naturally with other tools.

**Made for everyone.**  
Our goal is to make powerful software available across Linux, macOS and Windows wherever practical.
